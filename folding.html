Fold Imposer

Turn a 1-up PDF into press-ready outside and inside sheets for a folded leaflet.

3. Panel widths

Panels run left to right on the outside sheet. Leave a width blank to use the page width from the PDF.

Scaling keeps the proportions, so the page also gets slightly shorter.

4. Marks and bleed

Pages with a TrimBox are sized by it and bleed is taken from outside it, up to this amount. Pages without a TrimBox are treated as trim size plus this much bleed on every side. Edges that meet at a fold are trimmed back to the TrimBox.

5. Output

Sheet preview

Drag a page onto another panel to swap them. Double-click a page to turn it 180°.

Printing: double-sided, flip on the short edge for a landscape sheet, so the inside sheet backs up the outside. Check the first run against a folded dummy.
