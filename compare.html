How this works
Lists every ink on the page: the Cyan / Magenta / Yellow / Black plates (from CMYK, ICC-CMYK and gray fills and strokes), RGB content, and every spot colorant — including ones inside groups, patterns and image colour spaces. Uncheck an ink to hide it on the artwork preview: the file is rewritten with that ink zeroed in every colour operator (Form XObjects included) and re-rendered, so overprint, blends and transparency still composite correctly. Placed images are classified by their own colour space — a Separation/DeviceN image counts as its spot ink, never as CMYK — and are hidden or isolated as a whole. Limits: CMYK images (DeviceCMYK, ICC-CMYK, Indexed) are separated per plate, per pixel; a spot or multi-colorant DeviceN image is all-or-nothing and only disappears when all its inks are hidden; shading gradients can't be separated.
